Cuddleya

A calm baby log for iPhone and Apple Watch. Record sleep, feeds and diapers with a single tap, one-handed, even at night.

Coming to the App Store · currently in testing

The day at a glance

A day axis shows sleep, feeds and diapers in context, together with sunrise and sunset.

Keep it together

Invite your partner. You both see the same log, each on your own devices.

Apple Watch

Start and stop sleep or breastfeeding, or log a diaper, without reaching for your iPhone.

Growth and report

Track height and weight and create a PDF report, for example for the next doctor's visit.

Your data stays with you

Cuddleya has no user account, no server of its own, no ads and no tracking. Your entries live on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. We see none of it. Read more in the privacy policy.

Cuddleya is a diary, not a medical device. Hints such as the next sleep window are guidance drawn from your own entries and do not replace medical advice.
