Logging

One tap. Even one-handed.

Start a sleep or add it later, record a feed or a diaper. Press and hold the plus button for shortcuts.

Sleep

With a timer or afterwards, with details like place, sleep aid, pacifier and how your child slept, if you like.

Feeds

Nursing by side and duration, bottles in ml or fl oz, solids and pumping.

Diapers

Wet, stool or both, with color and consistency if you want.

Plus medication, measurements, notes, appointments and markers such as teething, vaccination or travel.

Today

Your day at a glance.

The day axis shows sleep, feeds and diapers together. Nights sit where it is dark outside: from sunset to sunrise if you allow your approximate location, otherwise from 7 p.m. to 7 a.m.

Below the axis you get numbers, not verdicts.

Forecast

How the forecast works.

  1. At first: age.While there are only a few entries, Cuddleya goes by what is typical for your child’s age.
  2. Then: your child.With every sleep you log, it follows your own rhythm more closely. The app shows you how many sleeps it is based on.
  3. Always: a window, not a fixed time.You see a likely time and a range around it. If the rhythm gets less settled, the app says so. If there will be no more naps today, one tap is enough.

Guidance, not a plan. Every day is different. The forecast begins at about eight weeks and does not replace medical advice.

iPhone and Apple Watch

On your wrist when your hands are full.

  • On Apple Watch, start and stop sleep and nursing, switch sides or log a diaper.
  • A running timer shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Stop button.
  • If you like, a notification reminds you about 30 minutes before the next likely sleep, not between 8 p.m. and 7 a.m.

Together

Keep the log together.

Share your child with your partner. You both see the same log on your own devices, on Apple Watch too. Entries made by the other person show their name; you can turn this off.

Invite through Apple’s iCloud sharing. Someone standing next to you can also open the invitation with a QR code. Sharing needs iCloud. Without iCloud it works too: on this iPhone only, without sharing.

Figures

A figure for every child.

Fifteen sleepy figures, drawn for Cuddleya. They sleep when your child sleeps.

Tap a figure: it falls asleep or wakes up. Pressed means: asleep.

PDF report

A report as a PDF, for example for the next doctor’s appointment, with every single entry if you like.

Growth

Record weight, length and head circumference and see them as percentiles.

Backups

Daily on your iPhone, optionally also in iCloud Drive. With a password, copies that leave your iPhone are encrypted.

History

Every entry, filterable by kind, with note search and a trash for mistakes.

Daily quote

A short saying on the Today screen each day. If you’d rather not, hide it for the day or turn it off in Settings.

First steps

A short tour on first launch that you can open again at any time.

Milestone card

At one, two and three weeks and then every month until the first birthday, a card with a photo, size and a few lines about your child, to share or print.

Privacy

Your data is yours.

Your entries live on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud or, for a shared child, in the iCloud of the person who shared it. We can’t access them.

  • No accountNo sign-up, no email address.
  • No server of oursThere is no Cuddleya server collecting entries.
  • No ads, no analyticsNo tracking in the app or on this website.
  • iCloud is optionalYou can turn sync off at any time.
